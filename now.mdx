## HARP

Working on a sustainable way to maintain [HARP](/projects/harp/).
[0.12](https://github.com/msqd/harp/releases/tag/0.12.0) is out: response
bodies now stream to the client, and the PostgreSQL and MySQL drivers became
optional extras. Also experimenting with Rust around HARP, looking for ways to
make it faster.

## Agentic factory

A lot of my time goes into my own tooling for running teams of coding agents:
how they plan, build, review and ship, and how each sprint makes the next one
better.

## Music

Piano: [pentatonics and quartal voicings](/wiki/music/pentatonics/),
open and closed. Diminished scales. Sixth-diminished block chords. Stride.
And the basics: technique, and time feeling.
