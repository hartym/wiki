## Kongo

[Kongo](/projects/kongo/) is an orchestrator and communication system for
coding agents. It makes multi-role teams cheap to assemble: autonomous agents
that talk to each other, each with its own goals, priorities and values.

Communication is the most expensive domain in any enterprise, so an enterprise built
out of agents needs it to be fluid.

## HARP

Working on a sustainable way to maintain [HARP](/projects/harp/). Also testing
[Rust web frameworks](/wiki/rust/web-frameworks/), to see how much faster it
can get.

## Music

Piano: [pentatonics and quartal voicings](/wiki/music/pentatonics/),
open and closed. Diminished scales. Sixth-diminished block chords. Stride.
And the basics: technique, and time feeling.
