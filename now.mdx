## Kongo

[Kongo](/projects/kongo/) is an orchestrator and communication system for
coding agents. It makes multi-role teams cheap to assemble: autonomous agents
that talk to each other, each with its own goals, priorities and values.

The conflicting interests are the point. Confronting them is what brings value
out of the conversations.

The thesis is simple. Communication is the most expensive domain in any
enterprise, so an enterprise built out of agents needs it to be fluid.

[cmux](/projects/cmux/) is the workspace they run in, one pane per git worktree.
[Plan de Scène](/projects/plan-de-scene/) is the first real application: a
small, highly interactive tool, built and maintained by three autonomous agents.

## Music

At the piano: [pentatonics and quartal voicings](/wiki/music/pentatonics/),
open and closed. Diminished usages. Sixth-diminished block chords. Stride.
A lot at once, and all of it foundation for the rest.

The most important part is none of that. It is the basics: technique, and time
feeling.
