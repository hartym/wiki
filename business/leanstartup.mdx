The Lean Startup methodology, developed by Eric Ries, is built around the Build-Measure-Learn feedback loop.

## The Build-Measure-Learn Loop

1. **Build** → Create a Minimum Viable Product (MVP)
2. **Measure** → Collect data on how customers use the product
3. **Learn** → Analyze data to validate or invalidate hypotheses

What the loop learns feeds the **pivot or persevere** decision. Ries recommends a regular meeting for it,
scheduled in advance: less than a few weeks apart is too often, more than a few months too rare.

## Key Concepts

* **Minimum Viable Product (MVP)** - The simplest version that allows you to start the learning process
* **Validated Learning** - The unit of progress: testing hypotheses through experiments with real customers
* **Innovation Accounting** - A kind of accounting specific to startups: how to measure progress, set milestones and prioritize work
* **Pivot** - A structured course correction designed to test a new fundamental hypothesis about the product, business model and engine of growth
