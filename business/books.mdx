## Zero to One (Peter Thiel)

<img 
  src="https://d.gr-assets.com/books/1414347376l/18050143.jpg" 
  alt="Zero to One (Peter Thiel)"
  style={{float: 'left', maxHeight: '200px', marginRight: '1rem', marginBottom: '1rem'}}
/>

[Zero to One](https://www.penguinrandomhouse.com/books/234730/zero-to-one-by-peter-thiel-with-blake-masters/),
by Paypal cofounder Peter Thiel, grew out of his 2012
Stanford CS183 course. A must-read on pure creation: going from 1 to n is a growth problem, going
from 0 to 1 is building something that did not exist before.

## The Four Steps to the Epiphany (Steve Blank)

<img 
  src="https://d.gr-assets.com/books/1347394098l/762542.jpg" 
  alt="The Four Steps to the Epiphany (Steve Blank)"
  style={{float: 'left', maxHeight: '200px', marginRight: '1rem', marginBottom: '1rem'}}
/>

[The Four Steps to the Epiphany](https://steveblank.com/books-for-startups/) is the book Steve Blank
wrote as the course text for his first entrepreneurship class, and where the customer development
vocabulary comes from. It reads
like a process, not an essay: customer discovery, customer validation, customer creation, company
building. The value is in the order, and in what each step has to prove before the next one starts.

Short version: <Link to="customerdevelopment" />.

## The Lean Startup (Eric Ries)

<img 
  src="https://d.gr-assets.com/books/1333576876l/10127019.jpg" 
  alt="Lean Startup (Eric Ries)"
  style={{float: 'left', maxHeight: '200px', marginRight: '1rem', marginBottom: '1rem'}}
/>

[The Lean Startup](https://theleanstartup.com/book) takes Blank's customer development and turns it
into a loop: build, measure, learn, then pivot or persevere. Most startups fail, and a good share of
those failures were preventable.

Short version: <Link to="leanstartup" />.

## Running Lean (Ash Maurya)

<img 
  src="https://d.gr-assets.com/books/1344712076l/13078769.jpg" 
  alt="Running Lean (Ash Maurya)"
  style={{float: 'left', maxHeight: '200px', marginRight: '1rem', marginBottom: '1rem'}}
/>

[Running Lean](https://www.oreilly.com/library/view/running-lean-3rd/9781098108762/) is the hands-on
one: the Lean Canvas, and a way to vet a product idea before spending months building the wrong
thing. Get the 3rd edition (O'Reilly, 2022).
