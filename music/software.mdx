## Digital Audio Workstations (DAW)

* Logic Pro: [Apple Logic Pro](https://www.apple.com/logic-pro/)
* Ableton Live: [Official Website](https://www.ableton.com/en/)
* Reason: [Reason Studios](https://www.reasonstudios.com/) (Propellerhead until 2019, hence the
  old links everywhere)

## Virtual instruments

* EZdrummer: [Toontrack EZdrummer](https://www.toontrack.com/product/ezdrummer-3/)

## Music Notation

* LilyPond: [GNU LilyPond](https://lilypond.org/), notation as source code, engraved by a compiler
* MuseScore: [Free Music Notation Software](https://musescore.org/en)
* OSMD: [Open Sheet Music Display](https://opensheetmusicdisplay.org/), renders MusicXML in a browser
* VexFlow: [Music Notation Rendering](https://www.vexflow.com/), draws notation from code, no MusicXML needed
