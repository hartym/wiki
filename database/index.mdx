Notes on database families and the engines in each. The families are a way to navigate, not a
recommendation to use one of each - most systems want a relational database and, occasionally, one
more thing for a workload it genuinely handles badly.

## Families

* <Link to="relational" /> - PostgreSQL, MySQL. The default, and usually the right one.
* <Link to="graphs" /> - Neo4j, JanusGraph, Dgraph, plus RDF and SPARQL. When the relationship is
  the data and the traversal depth is unbounded.
* <Link to="timeseries" /> - InfluxDB, TimescaleDB, Prometheus, Graphite. Timestamped,
  append-mostly.
* <Link to="keyvalue" /> - Redis, LevelDB. Split by whether it is a server or a library.
* <Link to="document" /> - MongoDB, CouchDB, Elasticsearch, Solr. Self-describing documents, and
  the search engines that index them.
* <Link to="gis" /> - PostGIS, coordinate systems, vector tiles.

## Engines and tools

* <Link to="postgresql" /> - the deep dive. Reaches into three of the families above through
  extensions.
* <Link to="presto" /> - Presto and Trino, distributed SQL over data you did not load.
