## Sequential

Dave Smith founded Sequential Circuits in San Francisco in 1974 and shipped the Prophet-5 in 1978.
He lost the brand, then got it back: **Yamaha returned the Sequential Circuits brands to him in
January 2015**, and Dave Smith Instruments rebranded as **Sequential on 31 August 2018**, the
Prophet-5's fortieth anniversary. Smith died on 31 May 2022; the company continues under the
Sequential name, and `davesmithinstruments.com` now redirects to
[sequential.com](https://sequential.com/).

* [Prophet-6](https://sequential.com/product/prophet-6/) - six-voice polyphonic analog, a
  reinterpretation of the Prophet-5 with effects.
* Mopho x4 - four-voice polyphonic analog, discontinued.
